🏥 Medical Waste — Why It Matters
🔬 What is Medical Waste?
Used syringes, needles, IV tubes, bandages, blood samples, expired medicine strips, surgical gloves, PPE kits, thermometers
☣️ Danger if Mixed with Regular Waste
Spreads HIV, Hepatitis B/C, infections. Injures waste-pickers. Contaminates groundwater and soil. Illegal under Bio-Medical Waste Rules, 2016.
✅ How to Dispose Safely
Seal in a puncture-proof RED or YELLOW bag. Use PURPLE bin (BIN-009 or BIN-010). Or call BBMP Bio-Medical helpline: 1800-425-0210.
🌟 Pre-Sorting Benefit — Why Separate at Home?
When you separate waste before coming to the bin:
✅ Govt saves ₹2–5 per kg in sorting labour
✅ Recyclables reach factory 2× faster, cleaner
✅ Compost is purer — better for farmers
✅ Medical waste treated safely — no infections
✅ You earn +25 extra points every time!
✅ Your area ranks higher → more BBMP funds

Rule of thumb at home:
Keep 4 small bins: 🔵 Dry / 🟢 Wet / 🔴 Dangerous / 🟣 Medical
